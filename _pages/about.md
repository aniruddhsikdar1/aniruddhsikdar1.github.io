I am currently a <strong>Postdoctoral Fellow</strong> at <a href="https://3ia.univ-cotedazur.eu" target="_blank">3IA Côte d'Azur</a>, working with the <a href="https://team.inria.fr/stars/" target="_blank">STARS team</a> at <a href="https://www.inria.fr/en/inria-centre-universite-cote-azur" target="_blank">Inria – Université Côte d'Azur</a>.

Previously, I was a <strong>Project Scientist-I</strong> at the Artificial Intelligence and Robotics Lab (AIRL), Indian Institute of Science (<a href="https://www.iisc.ac.in" target="_blank">IISc</a>), where I also served as a <strong>Co-Principal Investigator</strong> for an <a href="https://onlinecourses.nptel.ac.in/e-learning/preview/noc26_ee96" target="_blank">NPTEL course on AI-driven perception, learning, and mapping for drones</a> (<a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tQ7kX5Xpf70&list=PLgMDNELGJ1CZZLI8nL0sDXJ6vPOj5p4Hv&index=15" target="_blank">watch on YouTube</a>), focused on practical education in autonomous navigation and aerial robotics.

I have also worked as a <strong>Consultant</strong> at <a href="https://www.visist.ai/#/" target="_blank">Visist.AI</a>, developing computer vision solutions for noisy, high-speed environments, particularly in <strong>sports analytics</strong>, with an emphasis on robustness and real-time performance.

I earned my <strong>Ph.D.</strong> from the Robert Bosch Centre for Cyber-Physical Systems (<a href="https://cps.iisc.ac.in" target="_blank">RBCCPS</a>), IISc, under the supervision of <strong>Prof. Suresh Sundaram</strong> (<a href="https://www.linkedin.com/company/artificial-intelligence-and-robotics-laboratory/posts/?feedView=all" target="_blank">AIRL–IISc</a>). During my doctoral research, I also had the opportunity to work with <strong>Prof. <a href="https://ensundara.wordpress.com/biography/" target="_blank">Sundarrajan</a></strong>, <strong>Dr. <a href="https://chandan-iiti.github.io" target="_blank">Chandan Gautham</a></strong>, and <strong>Dr. <a href="https://www.arjunjain.co.in" target="_blank">Arjun Jain</a></strong>.

My broader research interests span <strong>computer vision, multimodal learning, domain adaptation, and robotics</strong>, with a focus on developing <strong>robust, efficient, and generalizable perception systems for real-world autonomous agents</strong>.
