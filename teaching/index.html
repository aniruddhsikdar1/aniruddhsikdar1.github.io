AI-Driven Perception, Learning and Mapping for Drones

COURSE 02

Advanced Perception for Autonomous Systems

Co-Principal Investigator Upcoming · NPTEL

This course provides an in-depth exploration of modern perception techniques for autonomous systems, with a focus on the latest advances in foundation models and efficient adaptation strategies for real-world deployment.

  • Large-scale foundation models for vision — architectures, pre-training paradigms, and zero-shot capabilities.
  • Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning (PEFT) — LoRA, adapters, prompt tuning, and their application to perception tasks.
  • Video understanding — temporal modelling, action recognition, multi-object tracking, and anomaly detection in video streams.
  • Vision-Language Actions (VLAs) — grounding language in visual perception for robotic planning and autonomous decision-making.

Topics at a Glance
