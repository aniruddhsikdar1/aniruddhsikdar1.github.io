🏆 Competitions & Challenges

Multi-modal Domain Fusion for Multi-modal Aerial View Object Classification

📄 Paper (arXiv) — Participant

  • Participated in the Multi-modal Aerial View Imagery Classification (MAVOC) Challenge at CVPR-W PBVS 2022 as a two-member team.
  • Developed a multi-modal deep learning framework that learns domain-invariant features from Electro-Optical (EO) and Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) labeled and unlabeled data.
  • Track 1 (EO + SAR test data, multi-modal case): Ranked 5th out of 82 teams.
  • Track 2 (SAR-only test data, missing modality case): Ranked 9th out of 77 teams.
  • Methodology published in:
    “Multi-modal aerial view object classification challenge results – PBVS 2022.”
    Proceedings of the IEEE/CVF Conference on Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition (CVPR) Workshops, 2022.

UAV RGB–IR Slant Angle Object Detection Challenge

🌎 Competition Page (Kaggle) — Organiser

  • Organized India’s first drone-based object detection competition using RGB and Infrared (IR) imagery on the proposed Indraeye dataset, promoting research in multi-spectral perception.
  • Hosted as part of the International Conference on Emerging Technology in Autonomous Aerial Vehicles (ETAAV) at IISc Bangalore.
  • Sponsored by SwaYaan (MeitY, Government of India).
