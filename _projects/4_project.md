Leveraging generative models — including diffusion models and image-to-image translation — to synthesize training data, augment scarce datasets, and improve model robustness across domains.

{% include figure.liquid loading="eager" path="assets/img/publication_preview/adain_final.drawio.png" title="SSL-RGB2IR Translation" class="img-fluid rounded z-depth-1" %}

**Key contributions:**
- **SSL-RGB2IR** (IROS 2024) — Semi-supervised RGB-to-IR image translation for boosting segmentation and detection model training.
- **Syn2Real** (IEEE GRSL 2025) — Diffusion-based synthetic side-scan sonar data generation for underwater object detection, demonstrating significant improvements over real-data-only baselines.
- Generative fused RGB–IR and RGB–SAR visualizations for Sim-to-Real aerial imagery analysis.
