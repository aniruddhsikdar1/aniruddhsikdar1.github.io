Developing cross-modal and multi-spectral deep learning frameworks that fuse information across RGB, infrared (IR), and SAR sensors for robust perception in challenging environments.

{% include figure.liquid loading="eager" path="assets/img/publication_preview/final11.drawio_updated.drawio.png" title="OGP-Net Architecture" class="img-fluid rounded z-depth-1" %}

**Key contributions:**
- **OGP-Net** (AAAI 2025) — Optical guidance with pixel-level contrastive distillation for robust multi-modal and missing-modality segmentation.
- **SKD-Net** (ICRA 2024) — Spectral-based knowledge distillation in low-light thermal imagery for robotic perception.
- **SSL-RGB2IR** (IROS 2024) — Semi-supervised RGB-to-IR image translation for enhancing segmentation and detection training.
- **AetherVision-Bench** (CVPRW 2025) — Open-vocabulary RGB-IR benchmark for multi-angle segmentation across aerial and ground perspectives.
- **SAGA** (CVPRW 2025) — Semantic-aware gray color augmentation for visible-to-thermal domain adaptation.
