**Key contributions:**
- **Fully Complex-Valued DL** (ICASSP 2023) — Fully complex-valued deep learning scheme for visual perception, demonstrating improved representational capacity over real-valued networks.
- **FC2MFN** (SSCI 2022) — Fully complex-valued fully convolutional multi-feature fusion network for building segmentation from InSAR imagery.
