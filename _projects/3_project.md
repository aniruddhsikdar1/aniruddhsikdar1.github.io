Developing robust target detection and tracking pipelines for dynamic real-world environments, spanning aerial surveillance, robotic platforms, and sports video analytics.

{% include figure.liquid loading="eager" path="assets/images/gta.drawio.png" title="Target Tracking" class="img-fluid rounded z-depth-1" %}

**Key contributions:**
- Multi-object tracking across drone and ground-based platforms using deep learning models.
- Racquet detection, shuttle tracking, and hit detection in fast-paced sports video at [Visist.AI](https://www.visist.ai/#/).
- Aerial object detection with robustness across varying slant angles and weather conditions.
- Evaluated SOTA detection models for aerial imagery analysis as part of a 3-year collaborative Sim-to-Real project (2022–2025).
